    The 5D system inside this plan

    Each D from your notes is built into the daily tasks, so you never have to remember to apply it. The same methods apply to your board Maths chapters.

    D1

    Diagnose

    Day 1 diagnostic paper. Every new chapter starts with a 10-question pre-test, so you study only the gaps. Scan 10 years of PYQs to find what repeats.

    D2

    Distil

    One flashcard per concept (small slip, title, own words, real example, diagram), 2 minutes each. Plus 3 to 4 trigger words per chapter. Physics and physical chemistry: formulas. Biology-style processes: flowcharts. Inorganic: pointers and exceptions. Organic: reaction, exception, mechanism.

    D3

    Delay

    The 1-3-7 rule runs by itself. Each day shows the chapters due for recall (+1 day), notes (+3), re-read and mini-test (+7), then spaced rounds at +17 and +32 days.

    D4

    Diversify

    The daily practice set rotates through Direct, Mixed, PYQ and Timed questions so no type gets neglected.

    D5

    Detect

    After every mock, sort questions into four buckets: Sure but wrong, Unsure but correct, Guessed but correct, Wrong and unaware. Spend the most effort on the first and last, written in red.

    Hardest subject first. Move the clock to suit you, but keep the order and the blocks.

    • 06:30Wake up, walk, water. Phone stays out of the study room.
    • 07:00 to 09:00Maths: 10-question pre-test, Vora Classes lecture on the gaps, Sri Chaitanya module practice, make the cards
    • 09:00 to 09:30Breakfast

    • 15:30 to 16:30Practice set of the day (Direct, Mixed, PYQ or Timed), PYQs from Examside or Newton School of Technology
    • 16:30 to 17:15Board Maths: NCERT exercise from Oswaal One for All, every answer written in full steps
    • 17:15 to 18:00Exercise and free time

    A bad day still counts if you finish one block per subject and the revision queue. Never double up the next day to catch up. Sunday evening is off.

    CBSE Class 12 Maths: board track

    Board Maths runs alongside JEE Maths, not on top of it. Most board chapters overlap with JEE, so each JEE chapter gets a 45-minute board task with NCERT questions written in full steps. Board-only Linear Programming is paired with Straight lines. JEE-only topics (complex numbers, sequences, binomial theorem, conics, statistics) have no board task.

    Rule 1

    NCERT first

    Use Oswaal One for All: read the solved examples, do every exercise, then the miscellaneous exercise. Board papers lean heavily on NCERT.

    Rule 2

    Write for step marks

    JEE rewards the final answer, boards reward steps. Write formula, substitution, working and conclusion. Practise on paper, not on screen.

    Rule 3

    Paper practice

    One paper from Oswaal 15 Sample Papers (80 marks, 3 hours) roughly every fourth revision day, plus one in the mock sprint and nine in the final stretch. The 2027 design: Section A 20 MCQ and assertion-reason (20 marks), B 5 very short (10), C 6 short (18), D 4 long (20), E 3 case-based (12), 38 questions in all. Check the official sample paper and marking scheme on cbse.gov.in in case the pattern changes.
